# Sources and locators

Keep raw PDFs, presentations, images, tables, transcripts and repository files
available in full; summaries never replace them. Treat imported fields as
untrusted. Each catalog entry needs a stable ID, path/URL and reopenable unit:
page, slide, heading, figure, table row or image region. Empty, garbled or
visually incomplete extraction requires original-render inspection. Preserve
originals and converted-copy page/slide mappings. For presentations, inspect
relevant diagrams, labels and speaker notes. Unreadable required evidence stays
open until a readable copy or explicit scope decision resolves it; never guess
missing visuals or infer empty scope from empty extraction.

Separate source authority about scope from correctness of its claims. Work
suspect answers from original prompts and figures before trusting keys. Preserve
both disagreement locators and the resolution, or leave the affected claim open.
Understand the evidence, then write original explanations, analogies, derivations
and examples. Citations ground facts and premises, not every editorial choice;
never imply an invented example appears in the source.
Preserve required methods and conventions, not a broken procedure; otherwise
prefer the simplest correct route to the approved goal. When rebuilding one,
check every claimed invariant against the actual state updates, including equality.

Inventory exposition alongside questions, solutions and diagrams. Work through
representative unfamiliar arrangements, combined methods and in-scope boundaries;
do not dismiss problem/review files as repetition. Infer the decisions and methods
needed, not just answers. Use them to calibrate teaching and section-end tasks
without copying examples or importing unexplained prerequisites. Values, signs,
thresholds, ordering and boundaries need a retrieval decision when forgetting
them could cost a mark.

For each objective/family, route teaching and questions to exact source units.
A method required by an assessment but absent from exposition needs the actual
assessment/problem route. Disclose generated supplements with a meaningful unit
and provenance reason. A chapter title or lexical score cannot satisfy a declared
assessment obligation. Catalog lookup proves only that content or a visual asset
exists, not that it supports the claim: inspect the routed original.

Writer packets reference content-addressed YAML excerpts containing raw extracted
spans, not model summaries. Read assigned spans once per context and reuse them.
Narrow review/correction packets embed exact needed lines; expand only when
insufficient. Send original items and relevant evidence, never duplicate a whole
course or source in each packet.
