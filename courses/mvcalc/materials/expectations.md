# Course Expectations: Multivariable Calculus

## Who This Course Is For
This course is designed for university students taking a standard Calculus III (Multivariable Calculus) course. It provides a complete, self-contained presentation of three-dimensional analytic geometry, vector-valued functions, multivariable differential calculus, multiple integration, and vector calculus through the divergence theorem.

## Prerequisites
Learners should have fluent, current command of:
- **Single-variable calculus (Calculus I–II)**: limits, derivatives, integrals, the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, substitution, integration by parts, trigonometric integrals, and basic improper integrals.
- **Parametric curves and polar coordinates**: plane parametric curves, converting between polar and rectangular coordinates, sketching polar curves, and the polar area formula.
- **Algebra and trigonometry**: completing the square, solving linear and nonlinear systems of equations, trigonometric identities, and the unit circle.

## Scope
The course covers Chapters 2 through 6 of *OpenStax Calculus Volume 3*:
- **Chapter 2: Vectors and the Geometry of Space** (vectors in 2D and 3D, dot product, cross product, lines and planes in space, quadric surfaces, cylindrical and spherical coordinates).
- **Chapter 3: Vector-Valued Functions** (curves in space, differentiation and integration of vector-valued functions, arc length, curvature, the TNB frame, and motion in space).
- **Chapter 4: Differentiation of Functions of Several Variables** (limits, continuity, partial derivatives, tangent planes, linear approximations, multivariable chain rules, directional derivatives, gradient vectors, extrema, and Lagrange multipliers).
- **Chapter 5: Multiple Integration** (double integrals in rectangular and polar coordinates, triple integrals in rectangular, cylindrical, and spherical coordinates, applications to mass and center of mass, and change of variables via Jacobians).
- **Chapter 6: Vector Calculus** (vector fields, line integrals, conservative fields, Green's theorem, divergence and curl, surface integrals, Stokes' theorem, and the Divergence theorem).

## Excluded
- OpenStax Chapter 1 (Parametric Equations and Polar Coordinates) is treated as established prerequisite knowledge rather than retaught from scratch.
- Formal <m>\epsilon</m>–<m>\delta</m> limit proofs in several variables.
- Advanced exterior calculus and differential forms beyond classical vector calculus.
